03 · Projects

Things I've built.

ExamOS

Honestly, I built it because I procrastinate. Every productivity app let me ignore it with one click. So I decided to build one that doesn't.

It's a layer that sits on top of your OS and forces you into study mode. Apps and websites you shouldn't be using get blocked. The only way to unblock them is to earn credits by actually studying.

The most extreme part is Deep Focus, a lockdown mode you genuinely can't escape. Alt+F4 doesn't work. Task Manager doesn't work. Even if you restart your PC, it resumes Deep Focus. It's for people like me who can't help but open their computer and get glued to it.

There's also an AI planner that reorganizes your study schedule when you inevitably fall behind, past paper management, a custom OS style UI, analytics tracking, gamification with XP and achievements, and support for different exam boards.

Built with

React TypeScript Tauri Rust NestJS PostgreSQL Prisma
